How it works

The pipeline behind every count.

🔍 1 · Detection

YOLOv8 (COCO-pretrained) scans every frame for cars, bikes, buses, trucks, bicycles and riders.

🧭 2 · Tracking

ByteTrack links detections across frames into stable per-vehicle tracks with IDs and trajectories.

📏 3 · Speed

Centroid motion × your pixels-per-metre calibration × fps = km/h per vehicle, smoothed over time.

🤖 4 · Fully automatic rules

Speed scale auto-calibrated from vehicle sizes (avg. car ≈ 1.8 m) · traffic-light colour read directly from the video, stop line placed at the light · allowed flow direction learned from where most vehicles move · rider without a detectable helmet. A manual-zone mode remains available for camera setups where auto fails.

🎬 5 · Output

Annotated video with boxes, speeds and badges, a timestamped violation log, evidence stills, CSV + PDF exports.

⚠️ Honest limits

Speed needs correct pixels-per-metre calibration. The traffic-light clock is user-defined (the app cannot see the real light unless you draw it in the video). Helmet detection is a colour heuristic and is indicative, not certified.
